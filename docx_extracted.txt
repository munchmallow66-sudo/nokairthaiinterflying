✈️ Dual-Career Pathway: Guaranteed Career Progression
Upon successful completion of the CPL program, graduates are offered a performance-based career pathway designed to secure your future in aviation
"Two Proven Pathways to the Nok Air Cockpit"
Our cadet program is structured to ensure that every successful graduate has a clear and secure path forward in their pilot career:
Direct Entry to Nok Air: Top-tier cadets who demonstrate exceptional proficiency will proceed directly into employment with Nok Air immediately upon graduation.
TIF Instructor & Priority Pipeline: Cadets who show strong foundational skills and instructional aptitude will be invited to join TIF as certified Flight Instructors. In this role, you will gain crucial flight hours, refine your command skills, and hold first-priority status for upcoming Nok Air pilot recruitment intakes.
Qualifications Cadet Pilot Program
1. Personal Information
- Thai nationality (male or female)
- Not more than 32 years of age as of the application closing date (Year of Birth from 1994 onwards)
- Male applicants must have completed military service or have been officially exempted
- Not currently bound by any training contract or commitment with another airline or aviation organization
- No criminal record or history of serious misconduct
2. Education & Language Proficiency  
- Bachelor's degree or higher in any field from an accredited institution
- Valid English language proficiency test result (issued within 2 years prior to the application date), meeting one of the following requirements:
- TOEIC score of 650 or above (Public Test only)
- IELTS Academic score of 5.5 or above
3.  Physical & Medical Requirements
- Minimum height:
- Male: 165 cm
- Female: 160 cm
- Physically and mentally fit to undertake professional pilot training.
- Eligible to obtain a CAAT Class 1 Medical Certificate.
- Applicants are responsible for ensuring that they meet the medical standards prescribed by CAAT. Details are available at: CAAT Class 1 Medical
- Applicants who have undergone LASIK or PRK surgery must have completed the procedure at least one year prior to the medical examination.
- No history of substance abuse, alcohol dependency, or psychiatric disorders.
 
Required Documents
1. Personal Resume/Curriculum Vitae (CV)
2. Thai National ID Card or a valid passport (must not be expired)
3. Name/Surname Change Certificate (if applicable)
4. Bachelor's Degree Certificate (or Graduation Certificate) and complete Academic Transcript
5. Proof of military service status (male applicants only)
   - Military Service Registration Certificate
   - Military Service Exemption Certificate (Sor Dor 8 or Sor Dor 43), or
   - For applicants currently serving, an official identification card or document confirming current military status
6. English language proficiency test result TOEIC 650 and above or IELTS 5.5 and above (issued within 2 years prior to the application date)7. Class 1 medical certificate (if any)
